THE SHARED FOUNDATION

One architecture.
Two expressions.

AUI means Absolute Universal Intelligence. It is the name of the authored architecture behind the Valkrais: a set of responsibilities such as creation, computation, command, control and coevolution.

Those identities inform serious software design and the characters and lore of the entertainment world. An architectural role is a direction for work, not proof of an implemented capability.
